# A place at the table

The bridge holds. On the far side, the house smells of cedar and rain.

An old woman opens the door before you knock. She sets a second cup on the table, as though you are only a little late.

“I wondered which way you would come,” she says.

You hang your coat beside the stove. Outside, the lantern goes out. For tonight, there is nothing left to find.
