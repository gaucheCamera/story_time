# What the river keeps

You follow the stones until the house is a small gold square behind you.

At the bend, the river opens into a lake. The clouds part. For the first time in years, you can see every star of a constellation whose name you have forgotten.

You sit on a flat stone and take off your shoes.

By morning, you will remember the way home. For now, it is enough to be here.
